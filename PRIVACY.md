# Privacy

> **Placeholder.** The full privacy policy will be written before the first release.

The app is being built to these rules (SPEC §3):

- No screen content from other apps (text, captions, usernames, images) is ever written to
  disk, logged in release builds, or sent over the network.
- No proprietary libraries, analytics, crash-reporting or ads SDKs.
- Everything works with no network connection. Friend battles are optional and off by
  default.
